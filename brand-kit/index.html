02Logo

The SX mark & lockup

The mark interlocks an S and an X, two paths that meet in one direction. Use the full lockup when there's room, and the mark alone for small spaces like avatars, favicons, and social icons.

Mark · PrimaryPNG ↓
Lockup · Full Stack Sales ConsultingPNG ↓

Official files from sarvx.io, used as supplied. Do not recolor or redraw.

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the width of the X stroke on every side
  • Use the files exactly as supplied: the mark keeps its white S and signal-blue X on the ink tile
  • On light pages, place the ink tile as-is (like the sarvx.io header)
  • Keep the lockup at least 120px wide on screen (1.25 in in print) and the mark at least 24px

Don't

  • Recolor, invert, or cut out the mark
  • Stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows or glows
  • Place it on busy photos without a solid or dimmed area behind it
  • Rebuild the wordmark in another font

05Voice

Calm, precise, evidence-led

SarvX sounds like a senior advisor: it states things plainly, backs claims with evidence, and focuses on outcomes. It never sounds like a hype seminar.

StructuredShort declarative sentences. Frameworks and steps over big adjectives.
AccountableTalk about measurement, ownership, and results the client can verify.
RespectfulHonest recommendations, real fit, no pressure. The buyer's decision is theirs.
Sounds like SarvX

"Diagnose the growth constraint. Align the operating system. Measure sustained performance."

"Great selling is leadership."

"You'll hear the full scope, pricing, and terms before any decision."

Doesn't sound like SarvX

"10X your income in 30 days!"

"Only 3 spots left — act NOW!"

"Revolutionary, game-changing secrets the gurus won't tell you."

06Design elements

Building blocks

Sharp 4px corners, thin blue-gray lines, generous white space, and a signature corner bracket that frames key content.

Corner bracket

Use the blue corner bracket to frame hero headlines, key stats, and featured quotes.

Photography

Architecture, glass, steel, and natural daylight with cool blue tones. Clean lines and a lot of sky. For people shots, use real, natural light and real work settings. Avoid posed stock handshakes.

Founder headshot: light blue backdrop (#C8D8E8). Match photo panels to that color so the portrait blends in.
