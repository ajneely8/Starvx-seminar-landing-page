The idea

Stop chasing the close. Start leading the conversation.

Most sales training teaches scripts and pressure. This training teaches you how to lead: prepare well, listen closely, ask better questions, and help buyers reach a clear decision they feel good about.

It works the same way on a car lot, a cold call, a front porch, or in a boardroom. You'll leave with a complete five-step sales process you can use on your very next conversation.
