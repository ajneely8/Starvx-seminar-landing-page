## 0. Rules before you post

1. **Keep the $100K/month figure out of ads and ad images.** Meta rejects income and "unrealistic outcome" claims. Use it only in organic posts, with the disclaimer.
2. **Don't call out personal attributes in Meta ads.** Write "For salespeople and business owners in Northern California", not "Are you struggling in sales?"
3. **Never mention the $5,000 + $2,500/mo implementation in promos.** Elias explains both prices live, only to qualified owners.
4. **Scarcity must be real.** "500 free seats" and "100 VIP seats" are real caps, and the landing page counts them down live. Don't claim a seat count that's lower than what the page shows.
5. **The live sign-up popups** only show real registrants who opted in. Never screenshot them or post them as testimonials.

Standard disclaimer for posts that mention revenue:
> *Results are Elias's own and are not typical or guaranteed. Your results depend on your offer, market, and effort.*
