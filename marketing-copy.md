## 0. Compliance rules (read before posting anything)

1. **Keep the $100K/month figure out of ads and ad images.** Meta rejects ads with income or "unrealistic outcome" claims, and it slows approval. Use it only on the landing page and in organic posts, and always with the disclaimer beside it.
2. **Don't name the viewer's personal attributes in Meta ads.** "Are you struggling in sales?" breaks policy. "For salespeople and business owners in Northern California" is fine.
3. **Don't mention the $5,000 + $2,500/mo implementation in any promo.** Elias's plan is to explain both prices together, in person, only to qualified owners. The promo line is "optional implementation conversation for qualified business owners."
4. **Don't fake scarcity.** Only say "limited seats" if a real cap exists (such as the VIP cap). Only show countdowns to the real start time.

Standard disclaimer (paste under any post that mentions revenue):
> *Results are Elias's own and are not typical or guaranteed. Your results depend on your offer, market, and effort.*
