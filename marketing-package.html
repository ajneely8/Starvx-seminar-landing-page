Before you post

Five rules that keep ads approved and keep the messaging honest.

No income claims in adsKeep "$100K/month" out of Meta ads and ad images. Use it only in organic posts, with the disclaimer.
No personal-attribute hooksWrite "For salespeople in Northern California", not "Are you struggling in sales?"
Implementation pricing stays live-onlyNever mention the $5,000 + $2,500/mo offer in promos.
Real scarcity only500 free and 100 VIP seats are real caps that count down on the page. Never claim fewer than it shows.
Fill the linkReplace [landing page URL]. Spanish ads use ?lang=es.
